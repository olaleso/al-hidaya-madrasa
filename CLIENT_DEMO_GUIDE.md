# Al-Hidaya landing-page demo

## Suggested five-minute walkthrough

1. Start with the headline: **Faith. Knowledge. Skills for every generation.**
2. Use the three pathway cards to explain the new structure:
   - Children’s Madrasah
   - Adult Qur’an Classes
   - Academic & Digital Skills
3. Open **Islamic & Arabic Studies** and switch between the Qur’an, Arabic and Islamic Studies tabs.
4. Select a course such as **Ten Qira’at** to demonstrate the pre-selected registration form.
5. Show the adult men-and-women pathway and the community learning cards.
6. Finish with the existing secure portal link to demonstrate that the public redesign and management system remain connected.

## Content decisions for client approval

- “SAT/GCE” is presented as **SATs & GCSE Tutorials** for a UK audience.
- “Hifza” is presented as **Hifz**.
- “Ahadith” and “Towhid” are presented as **Hadith Studies** and **Tawhid**.
- Timetables, fees, age ranges and teacher details are intentionally omitted until the centre confirms them.
- Adult classes are described as separate options for men and women, subject to programme availability.

## Scope of this version

This is a clickable public-page concept. Existing portal and admission services are preserved. The wider course catalogue uses the current admissions endpoint as an expression-of-interest form; a dedicated multi-programme backend can be designed after the client approves the public structure.
